# VISION

## The Modern Village

Thresh is an open-source social infrastructure engineered to re-establish the "village." It aims to confront the modern epidemic of social isolation, superficial swiping algorithms in dating, and the solitary burdens faced by single parents and individuals with disabilities.

### Foundational Concepts
- **Action-Based Matchmaking:** Connecting individuals through shared life priorities, real-world helpfulness, and practical acts of service.
- **Radical Helpfulness:** Recognizing everyone has something to give, and everyone has areas where they need a hand. It's mutual aid without charity or pity.
- **Safety First:** Ensuring deep protection against exploitation and predation. The architecture fundamentally requires reciprocity and clear intent through anti-exploitation guardrails and mandatory verification.
- **Physical Presence:** Encouraging relationships to begin and grow through in-person, low-stakes activities rather than just virtual chat.

## User-Satisfaction Design
*   **Accessible First:** Designed heavily for users with motor or cognitive variations, achieving WCAG AAA standards.
*   **Warm Identity:** UI centered around warm, organic earth tones rather than neon tech-focused palettes.
*   **Genuine Media:** Focusing on action-based, real-life photography and interaction rather than polished selfies.
